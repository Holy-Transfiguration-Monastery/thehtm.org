# How to update thehtm.org

You edit the website at **https://app.pagescms.org**, signed in with your own GitHub account. Ask the site administrator for an invitation.

Changes appear on the live site **about one minute** after you click **Save**. Every change is kept, so a mistake can always be undone; just ask the administrator.

---

## Add a news post

1. Open **News** in the left menu and click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and **Date**.
3. Write the text. Use the toolbar for bold, lists and links. To add a picture, click the image button and upload it.
4. Click **Save**.

Spiritual Reading works the same way.

## Add a hymn (PDF) to a music page

1. Open **Media** → **Music PDFs and documents** and upload the PDF.
   - Use a clear file name, for example `sept-8-birth-of-the-theotokos.pdf`.
2. Open **Music pages** and choose the page, for example "Dismissal Hymns and Kontakia for September".
3. In **Hymns on this page**, type the hymn's name where it belongs.
4. Select the text, click the **link** button and pick the PDF you uploaded.
5. Click **Save**.

## Add a new music page

1. Open **Music pages** and click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Page title**
   - **Section**: English or Greek
   - **Appears under**: the page it belongs to, for example a month under "Menaion Hymns"
   - **Position in its list**
3. Add the hymns as described above, then click **Save**.

The new page is added to the Music menu automatically.

## Add a photo album

1. Open **Photo albums** and click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in the **Album title**.
3. Under **Photos**, click **Add** for each picture, upload it and add a caption if you like.
4. Choose a **Cover photo** and click **Save**.

## Change the address, phone numbers or menu links

Open **Contact details and links**, make the change and click **Save**.

---

**Tips**

- Phone photos are large. Pictures under about 2 MB load faster for visitors.
- If something looks wrong after saving, don't worry. The previous version is always kept.
