_The Second Edition of The Ascetical Homilies of St Isaac the Syrian on the press. Lord willing, we will receive copies in two weeks._

Last week the Ascetical Homilies were printed. Yesterday, September 29, we received and approved sample copy of the cover. Today we received a copy of the unbounded folded signatures of the printed book for approval. The final books should be here within a couple of weeks.
