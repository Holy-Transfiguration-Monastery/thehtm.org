_Holy Week_

This book offers the services from Lazarus Saturday through Holy Saturday translated into English from the Greek Triodion. It is formatted to match the size and format of the twelve volumes of our Menaion and our Pentecostarion printed in 2014.  
In the coming years we also intend to print the full Triodion and a bilingual personal edition of Holy Week in smaller format. Since that will take a good deal of time, we are offering Holy Week now. Because it is much shorter than the full Triodion, we have been able to include the full texts of all the New Testament readings, including the Twelve Passion Gospels of Holy Friday Matins. We have also provided the full service of the Six Psalms for Holy Monday for the convenience of those performing the Bridegroom Services.  
It includes a full-color double frontispiece with the Raising of Lazarus and the Crucixion.
